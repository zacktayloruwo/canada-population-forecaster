## Fertility and mortality

Fertility rates are averaged over your selected reference period. Each cohort's expected five-year birth total is split evenly between male and female newborns — a simplifying 50/50 assumption; the true sex ratio at birth is closer to 105 boys per 100 girls.

Mortality uses the three-year life table closest to each reference period: 2006–11 → the 2010/2012 table, 2011–16 → 2015/2017, and 2016–21 → 2019/2021, which captures the pandemic's mortality effect.
