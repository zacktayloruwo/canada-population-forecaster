## Base-year and historical population

The 2021 starting population is Statistics Canada's annual intercensal estimate (July 1), not a raw census count — the estimates program reconciles census counts with vital statistics and migration. The historical "Actual" line is drawn at each census year from 1981 to 2016 and is deliberately not connected to the 2021 projection, to keep real history visually distinct from the forecast.

Nunavut split from the Northwest Territories in 1999, so its historical line starts in 1991, the earliest year the two are reported separately. Historical % foreign-born comes from census files (via the UNI-CEN project) that are not adjusted for net undercoverage, so those values sit on a slightly different population base than the other historical series.
