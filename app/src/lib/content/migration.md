## International migration and the foreign-born population

Net international migration = immigrants − emigrants + returning emigrants, averaged over the reference period, with that period's age, sex, and provincial settlement profile. Both the current-trends line and the slider's default use the fixed 0.945%-of-population policy rate rather than each period's own observed average, so the two scenarios start identical whichever reference period you pick.

The 2021 foreign-born stock comes from the census in 10-year age bands, which are distributed across the model's 5-year cohorts in proportion to each province's general population shape — a simplification. Immigrants are assumed to have the same age-specific fertility and mortality as the general population, and non-permanent residents are excluded throughout.
