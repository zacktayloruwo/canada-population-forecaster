## What the forecast is — and is not

A forecast like this is a **conditional projection**: it shows what would happen *if* the rates you chose held for fifty years. It is not a prediction. Small annual differences compound — a quarter-point change in the immigration rate moves the 2071 population by millions — which is exactly why exploring scenarios side by side is more informative than any single number. The tool is intended to illustrate the methods and dilemmas of long-term population forecasting and their policy implications, not to supplant official projections.
