## Data sources at a glance

| Component | Source | Role in the model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base population (2021) | StatCan Table 17-10-0005-01, population estimates on July 1 by age and gender | Starting stock for every province × age × sex cohort |
| Historical series (1981–2016) | Table 17-10-0005-01; census files processed by the UNI-CEN project (% foreign-born) | The "Actual" line on the time-series chart |
| Fertility | Table 13-10-0418-01, age-specific fertility rates by province | Births added to the 0–4 cohorts each step |
| Mortality | Life tables 13-10-0114-01 (complete) and 13-10-0140-01 (abridged: PE, YT, NT, NU) | Survival probabilities by age and sex |
| Foreign-born stock (2021) | Table 98-10-0349-01, place of birth by gender and age, 2021 Census | Baseline for the % foreign-born indicator |
| International migration | Table 17-10-0014-01, components of international migration by age and gender | Age–sex–province profile of net newcomers |
| Interprovincial migration | Tables 17-10-0015-01 (age/sex of movers) and 17-10-0022-01 (origin–destination flows) | Flow matrix behind the internal-migration scenarios |
| Seat allocation | Constitution Act, 1867, ss. 51(1) and 51A; Elections Canada | Decennial redistribution of Commons seats |
