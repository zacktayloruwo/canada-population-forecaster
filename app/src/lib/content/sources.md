## Data sources at a glance

| Component | Source | Role in the model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base population (2021) | StatCan [Table 17-10-0005-01](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1710000501), population estimates on July 1 by age and gender | Starting stock for every province × age × sex cohort |
| Historical series (1981–2016) | [Table 17-10-0005-01](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1710000501); census files processed by the [UNI-CEN project](https://observatory.uwo.ca/unicen) (% foreign-born) | The "Actual" line on the time-series chart |
| Fertility | [Table 13-10-0418-01](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1310041801), age-specific fertility rates by province | Births added to the 0–4 cohorts each step |
| Mortality | Life tables [13-10-0114-01](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1310011401) (complete) and [13-10-0140-01](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1310014001) (abridged: PE, YT, NT, NU) | Survival probabilities by age and sex |
| Foreign-born stock (2021) | [Table 98-10-0349-01](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=9810034901), place of birth by gender and age, 2021 Census | Baseline for the % foreign-born indicator |
| International migration | [Table 17-10-0014-01](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1710001401), components of international migration by age and gender | Age–sex–province profile of net newcomers |
| Interprovincial migration | Tables [17-10-0015-01](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1710001501) (age/sex of movers) and [17-10-0022-01](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1710002201) (origin–destination flows) | Flow matrix behind the internal-migration scenarios |
| Seat allocation | [Constitution Act, 1867](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/const/), ss. 51(1) and 51A; [Elections Canada](https://www.elections.ca/content.aspx?section=res&dir=cir/red/allo&document=index&lang=e) | Decennial redistribution of Commons seats |
